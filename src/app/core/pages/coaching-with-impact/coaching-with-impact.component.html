Coaching With Impact Resource

Every coach has a certain level of influence both on and off the field. How a coach leverages this influence is critical. Coaching with Impact is designed for coaches to come alongside each other in the context of a small group and maximize the influence God has given them to multiply disciples in the world of sports.

Coaching with Impact will teach coaches how to coach and lead with the Character and Conduct of Christ, so they can in turn reproduce those same principles in the lives of other coaches.

First “Competing with Impact” High School Discipleship Group at ECHS

What an incredible moment to witness. We celebrated the very first Competing with Impact high school discipleship group at East Coweta High School, led by none other than Coach Mark Bowles.

Coach Mark, who was discipled by Kevin Burrell, is now stepping into leadership and pouring into the next generation of disciples. It’s amazing to see the impact of this mentorship come full circle — Mark is now leading his own group, making disciples, and empowering students to grow in their faith.

Excited to see how God continues to move through this group and the lives of these students. Let’s keep praying for the students of ECHS and leaders like Coach Mark Bowles, who are living out the call to make disciples.
