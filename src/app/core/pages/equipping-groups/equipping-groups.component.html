OUR APPROACH TO EQUIPPING

Impact Discipleship Equipping Groups are designed to help pastors and church leaders rediscover and live out Jesus’ original vision for the church—a movement of multiplying disciples.

Drawing from over 30 years of real-life ministry experience, these small-group learning environments invite leaders to study the life and methods of Jesus alongside other like-minded pastors and leaders.

Through short videos, focused articles, and live Zoom discussions, each equipping group provides practical, biblical insight you can immediately apply in your local church.

It’s a simple investment that produces lasting impact—helping you lead, build, and multiply disciples the way Jesus intended.

Impact Discipleship offers three Equipping Group courses.

Disciple-Making Church
Designed for churches ready to move from good intentions to intentional disciple-making. This equipping group helps pastors and key leaders align their church around a shared mission, model, and practical pathway to build a sustainable disciple-making culture—together.
Disciple-Making Pastor
Created specifically for pastors who want to lead with greater clarity, confidence, and multiplication. Rooted in 30+ years of real-world experience, this equipping group focuses on the six priorities every Disciple-Making Pastor must embrace to lead their congregation toward a movement of disciples who make disciples.
Disciple-Making Leader  
Built for everyday leaders who want to live out and lead the mission of making disciples in their ministry context. This equipping group focuses on personal spiritual growth, practical disciple-making tools, and faithfulness over the long haul—no formal ministry role required.
