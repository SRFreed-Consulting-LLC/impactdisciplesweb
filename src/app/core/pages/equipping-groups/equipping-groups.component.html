OUR APPROACH TO EQUIPPING

Impact Discipleship Equipping Groups are designed to help pastors, church leaders, and everyday disciple-makers align their lives and ministries around Jesus’ mission of making disciples.

Built on more than 30 years of real-world disciple-making experience, each online equipping group offers a clear, practical pathway for leading with greater purpose, faithfulness, and multiplication.

The course consists of seven online sessions where pastors meet with a group of twelve peers to discuss the strategic content developed by our team. Pastors will receive personalized coaching and accountability throughout the course to help them develop the necessary skills to fulfill Jesus’ mission of making disciples.

Through live Zoom sessions, curated reading and video training, guided discussion, coaching, and actionable tools, participants are equipped in community to move from intention to implementation.

Whether you are leading a church, shepherding a ministry, or influencing others as a leader, these equipping groups are relational, accessible, and immediately applicable—helping you build a disciple-making culture that lasts.

Impact Discipleship offers three Equipping Group courses.

Disciple-Making Church
Designed for churches ready to move from good intentions to intentional disciple-making. This equipping group helps pastors and key leaders align their church around a shared mission, model, and practical pathway to build a sustainable disciple-making culture—together.
Disciple-Making Pastor
Created specifically for pastors who want to lead with greater clarity, confidence, and multiplication. Rooted in 30+ years of real-world experience, this equipping group focuses on the six priorities every Disciple-Making Pastor must embrace to lead their congregation toward a movement of disciples who make disciples.
Disciple-Making Leader  
Built for everyday leaders who want to live out and lead the mission of making disciples in their ministry context. This equipping group focuses on personal spiritual growth, practical disciple-making tools, and faithfulness over the long haul—no formal ministry role required.
