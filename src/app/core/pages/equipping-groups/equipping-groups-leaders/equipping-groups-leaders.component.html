The Disciple-Making Leader Equipping Group is a brand-new online training experience designed to equip everyday leaders in the local church to live out and lead the mission of making disciples.
It all begins with you—deepening your personal and spiritual growth to build a ministry that truly impacts your church and community. If you want to make and multiply disciples in your ministry, deciding to enroll in this group could be life‑changing.
A Disciple-Making Leader:
  1. Knows the Mission - Understands God’s heart and purpose for making disciples.
  2. Models the Mission - Lives out disciple-making through personal obedience and example.
  3. Executes the Mission - Takes intentional steps to disciple others in everyday life and ministry.
  4. Multiplies the Mission - Helps disciples become disciple-makers.
  5. Protects the Mission - Guards the focus and values of disciple-making within the church.
  6. Finishes the Mission - Perseveres faithfully, passing the mission on to the next generation.
  • * Live online sessions designed for church leaders at every level
  • * Practical teaching rooted in real-life disciple-making
  • * Guided discussion and application in community
  • * Simple, reproducible tools leaders can use immediately
  • * Ongoing encouragement and support for long-term faithfulness

  • * Ministry leaders and team leaders
  • * Volunteers and emerging leaders
