Register for Summit

From its start in 2021, the Disciple-Making Summit has had a singular mission — calling disciple-makers, leaders and pastors across the nation and around the world to come together and focus on our main purpose. This is a weekend for everyone. Bring yourself, your small group, your youth group, your church staff — you will leave inspired and better equipped to make disciples in the coming year.
